SOUND IDEAS COURSE-LIBRARY NOTICE

Some Quack Kitchen sound effects are derived from the Sound Ideas General HD
SFX Collection and Cartoon Express SFX Library. Embedded audio metadata names
these collections and Sound Ideas as the publisher.

DigiPen Institute of Technology Singapore provides the Sound Ideas Library in
its Games Studio labs for students in GAM and PRJ courses. Its published usage
notice says student project work may be displayed internally, externally on
the internet, and at festivals or other events, and that students must use
assets generated by DigiPen students/staff or purchased and owned by DigiPen.

Governing DigiPen Singapore notice and agreement:

https://it-faq.sg.digipen.edu/Softwares/Music/Sound_Ideas_Library/index.html

Sound Ideas describes its products as royalty-free synchronization libraries:

https://www.sound-ideas.com/

This notice records the course-provided source of the assets; it does not
grant rights beyond the institutional and publisher terms.
