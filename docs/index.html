The story

One duck, one restaurant, five days

You have never run the place before. Your parents have gone on holiday anyway. Orders come in from the counter, ingredients need chopping and cooking, and every customer has a temper that runs on a timer.

Six kitchens teach you the job and then stop being polite about it. Three stars per day, if you can keep up.

Cook the whole dish

Fetch, chop, fry and plate. Every order is built from its parts, and a wrong plate goes in the bin.

Customers who wait, then leave

Take the order at the counter, seat them, serve them. Take too long and they walk, and it costs you.

Six kitchens

Five days and the tutorials between them, each with a longer menu and a busier floor.

Three stars a day

Every day scores you against three marks, so a day you cleared is still a day you can beat.

Keyboard or gamepad

Play with WASD and two keys, or on a controller. Menus take either, and a pad can be plugged in mid-game.

Its own engine

Written in C++ on the team's DuckEngine, with OpenGL, GLFW, FreeType and FMOD.

How it plays

Two keys and a direction

Walk up to a station, pick things up, and hold to chop. The in-game journal has the same pages, whenever you need them.
