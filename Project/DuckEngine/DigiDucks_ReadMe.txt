Game Concept:
Quack Kitchen is a 2D top-down cooking-simulation game where players play as a duck who is managing a restaurant for the first time.

Editor Demo Input:
Press P to turn on/off debug draw

Game Demo Controls:
WASD to move around.
ESC in Game to move to Main Menu.
Press J to interact with the surrounding items.
Hold K on either the chopping board or stove to use.

Cheats:
Press V to make a Salad. (Must not be holding anything)
Press B to make Cheese Burger. (Must not be holding anything)
Press N to increase the score by 10.
Press M to go to Score End Screen.
