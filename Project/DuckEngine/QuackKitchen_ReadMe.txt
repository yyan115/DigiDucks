Game Concept:
Quack Kitchen is a 2D top-down cooking-simulation game where players play as a duck who is managing a restaurant for the first time.

Game Controls:
WASD to move around.
Press R to interact with the surrounding items
Hold T on either the chopping board or stove to use.
